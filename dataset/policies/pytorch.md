# AI Policy: PyTorch

- **Project**: PyTorch
- **Ecosystem / Category**: Machine Learning Framework / Python & C++
- **Source URL**: https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/blob/main/AI_POLICY.md
- **Reference Document**: PyTorch AI Policy (`AI_POLICY.md`)
- **Primary Stance**: Restricted

## Verbatim Policy Text

> "Any AI-generated content in comments, issues, or PRs must be clearly disclosed and contained under a specific section heading like 'Agent details', or wrapped inside a code/quote block.
>
> AI-generated content must always be accompanied by human commentary explaining its relevance rather than pasted raw.
>
> Contributors must fully understand, review, and take full responsibility for any code, text, or analysis they submit. Fully autonomous or unsupervised agent-generated contributions are prohibited.
>
> Unreviewed or raw AI output should not be used to respond to comments or questions."

## 4-Dimension Breakdown

### 1. Usage Regulations
- **Overall Level**: `restricted`
- **Scope**: Core framework code, kernels, tests, documentation, and discussion threads.
- **Exceptions**: Allowed with explicit containment and human commentary. Fully autonomous unsupervised bots are forbidden.

### 2. Disclosure Requirements
- **Mandatory Disclosure**: Required across all PRs, issues, and comments.
- **Method**: Section header "Agent details" or wrapped inside markdown quote/code blocks with accompanying human explanation.

### 3. Comprehension & Accountability
- **Human Comprehension**: Required. Contributor must fully understand, review, and defend the patch.
- **Legal Accountability**: Subject to Linux Foundation / PyTorch CLA terms.

### 4. Review & Issue Rules
- **Automated PRs / Issues**: Autonomous PRs without human sponsorship are closed.
- **Automated Reviews**: Unreviewed or raw AI output cannot be used to reply to maintainers or questions.

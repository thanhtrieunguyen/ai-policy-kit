# AI Policy: OpenStreetMap (OSM)

- **Project**: OpenStreetMap (OSM)
- **Ecosystem / Category**: Geospatial Data & Mapping Community / Web & C++
- **Source URL**: https://community.openstreetmap.org/
- **Reference Document**: OSM Community Forum Moderation Policy on Chatbots & AI Slop
- **Primary Stance**: Restricted

## Verbatim Policy Text

> "Moderators are empowered to address or summarily remove content deemed 'beyond a reasonable doubt' to be low-value, unedited generative AI text ('AI slop') that degrades the signal-to-noise ratio of human-centered discussions.
>
> Thoughtful, human-driven use of AI tools (translation for non-native speakers, query syntax) requires appropriate disclosure and context.
>
> Automated scraping, bulk data ingestion, or unmonitored script/bot usage directed at core OSM infrastructure remain strictly governed by API Usage Policies."

## 4-Dimension Breakdown

### 1. Usage Regulations
- **Overall Level**: `restricted`
- **Scope**: Forum discussions, changeset comments, map feature tagging, documentation.
- **Exceptions**: Thoughtful assistive use (such as translation or query syntax) permitted with disclosure.

### 2. Disclosure Requirements
- **Mandatory Disclosure**: Required when AI is used to formulate forum comments or automated queries.
- **Context Obligation**: Human commentary must provide real context.

### 3. Comprehension & Accountability
- **Human Comprehension**: Required. Human mappers and community members are responsible for ground truth accuracy.
- **Legal Accountability**: Open Database License (ODbL) compliance; copyright integrity of mapped data.

### 4. Review & Issue Rules
- **Discussion Moderation**: Moderators summarily delete unedited AI-generated forum posts and comments.
- **Bot Ingestion**: Unmonitored bot scripts directed at OSM APIs are blocked.

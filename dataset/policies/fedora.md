# AI Policy: Fedora Project

- **Project**: Fedora Project
- **Ecosystem / Category**: Linux Distribution / Red Hat Ecosystem
- **Source URL**: https://docs.fedoraproject.org/en-US/council/policy/ai-contribution-policy/
- **Reference Document**: Fedora Council Policy on AI-Assisted Contributions
- **Primary Stance**: Restricted / Permissive

## Verbatim Policy Text

> "Human contributors take full personal responsibility for anything they submit (MUST). AI-generated outputs are treated strictly as suggestions.
>
> When a significant portion of a contribution is taken directly from an AI tool without human modification, it must be disclosed (MUST) using commit trailers such as Assisted-by: <tool/model>.
>
> AI tools cannot be used as the sole or final decision-makers when evaluating substantive human contributions or reviewing changes (MUST NOT).
>
> Dumping unverified AI-generated text ('AI slop') into Ask Fedora, bug trackers, or mailing lists is prohibited."

## 4-Dimension Breakdown

### 1. Usage Regulations
- **Overall Level**: `restricted`
- **Scope**: AI allowed across code, packaging, and documentation provided the contributor validates the output and retains human ownership.
- **Exceptions**: Permissive for iterative assistance; strict bans on unedited "AI slop" dumps.

### 2. Disclosure Requirements
- **Mandatory Disclosure**: Required when significant portions of work are directly generated by AI.
- **Commit Trailer**: Standard trailer `Assisted-by: <tool/model>`.
- **Wiki / Docs Tagging**: Specific wiki category tag `[[Category:AI Assistance Used]]`.

### 3. Comprehension & Accountability
- **Human Comprehension**: Required. Contributor must personally understand the submission and act as the sole author of record.
- **Legal Accountability**: Full adherence to Fedora Contributor Agreement (FPCA) and open-source licenses.

### 4. Review & Issue Rules
- **Automated PRs / Issues**: Banned from flooding Bugzilla or Ask Fedora forums.
- **Automated Reviews**: AI cannot be used as sole or final arbiter when reviewing human contributions.
